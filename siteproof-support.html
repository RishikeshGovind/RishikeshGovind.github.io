Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my company details to reports?

Open Settings in the app and fill in your company name and contact under "Company / Contractor Details." These are saved and used on the reports you generate. Branded report details are part of SiteProof Pro.

Why does my report show "—" for contractor?

That line is filled from your saved company details. Add them in Settings. They appear on reports when you are subscribed to SiteProof Pro.

Are my photos backed up?

Photos are stored on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, synced to your own private iCloud account so they are available across your devices. Your data is not stored on any developer server.

Can I add photos I already took outside the app?

Yes. Use "Import from Library." Imported photos are clearly marked, and their date and location are read from the photo's own metadata where available.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. In the app, go to the subscription screen and tap "Manage Subscription," or open the App Store app → your account → Subscriptions.

How do I free up storage?

Open Storage in the app to see usage, clear the thumbnail cache, compress archived project photos, and export projects before removing them.
