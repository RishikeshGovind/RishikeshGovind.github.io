01 / Problem

Most job boards show the same listings

Indeed and LinkedIn aggregate broadly, but they miss a significant slice of the market: roles posted directly on company career pages, smaller ATS platforms like Greenhouse and Lever, and niche boards that cater to specific industries. Candidates relying only on the major platforms are searching a fraction of what's actually available.

02 / Approach

Direct from source — before it reaches the big platforms

RoleUp aggregates from company career sites, applicant tracking systems, and niche boards, then surfaces those listings in a clean native interface. If a company posted a role somewhere online, there's a good chance RoleUp has it.

Search works across title, skill, and company name. Filters cover location, remote, employment type, how recently it was posted, and job categories including tech, healthcare, finance, design, and marketing. Salary ranges are shown where available so you know what to expect before applying.

Saved jobs and searches persist between sessions via Core Data. For supported listings, Easy Apply lets you submit directly in the app — add your details once, attach a resume, and apply without redirects or repeated form-filling. Company pages show all open roles for a specific employer in one place.

03 / Result

A job search advantage through better source coverage

RoleUp gives job seekers visibility into listings most people searching right now will never find. By going directly to the source rather than waiting for aggregators to catch up, users see roles earlier and face less competition. The in-app apply flow removes the friction of jumping between apps and re-entering the same information repeatedly.
