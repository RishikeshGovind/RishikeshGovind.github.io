Salary Data

01 / Problem

Job data is siloed across dozens of ATS platforms and job boards

A developer building a job search product, recruiting tool, or labour market dashboard needs to integrate with Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Adzuna, Reed, and half a dozen other platforms — each with its own auth flow, rate limits, pagination scheme, and response schema. The result is months of integration work just to get to a unified dataset, and the integrations break every time a source updates its API.

02 / Approach

Real-time parallel queries across 10+ sources, deduplicated into one response

Job Listings API fans out search queries in parallel across Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, BambooHR, Reed, Adzuna, and additional sources — deduplicating results and normalising them into a single consistent response schema. Filters for job title, location, employment type, and experience level apply across all sources simultaneously. Individual job detail endpoints return full descriptions, requirements, and direct application URLs.

Beyond raw listings, the intelligence layer adds three premium data layers: salary insights with p25, median, and p75 rates broken down by title, location, and experience level; employer profiles with exact open role counts and average advertised salaries; and market statistics covering top hiring companies, trending roles by listing frequency, and skill demand by region and industry. All data reflects live market state, not stale estimates.

03 / Result

One endpoint replacing a dozen fragile job board integrations

Job boards, recruiting platforms, and career tools can ship real-time job search in a single API call without managing source-specific integrations. The salary and market data layers turn a simple job feed into a labour market intelligence platform. Deployed on Railway with instant API key registration via the auth endpoint and tiered plans on RapidAPI — from a free tier for prototyping to volume plans with overage pricing for high-traffic production apps.
