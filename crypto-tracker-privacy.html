Privacy Policy

App: Crypto Tracker
Effective date: May 4, 2026

Crypto Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may collect or access, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information the App Uses

The app may use the following types of information to provide its features:

  • Account information, such as your email address and login credentials, when you create an account or sign in.
  • App configuration data, such as watchlists, saved alerts, tracked wallet addresses, and related preferences.
  • Subscription status, through Apple StoreKit, to determine whether paid access is active.
  • Notification-related data, including a device push token if notifications are enabled, so the app can support alert delivery.
  • Wallet and market query data, such as wallet addresses or tokens you look up, to fetch crypto market, chart, and wallet activity information.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to operate and support the app experience, including to:

  • authenticate your account,
  • save your tracked tokens, alerts, and wallet monitoring preferences,
  • deliver market intelligence, wallet activity, and chart-based features,
  • send notifications and price alert updates if you enable them, and
  • verify, restore, or manage subscription access.

3. Data Storage

Some app data is stored locally on your device, including certain preferences and cached app state. Sensitive tokens or credentials may be stored securely using Apple-provided secure storage mechanisms such as the Keychain.

Some account-related and feature-related data may also be transmitted to and stored on the app's backend services in order to support login, syncing, alerts, and subscription-linked access.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Crypto Tracker may send notifications related to price alerts, tracked wallet activity, whale or market events, and other app updates. You can disable notifications at any time in your device Settings.

5. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your full payment card information. The app may use purchase status and transaction information made available by Apple to determine access to paid features.

6. External Content and Links

The app may open external news articles, research pages, or web content inside an in-app browser or through system web views. When you visit external websites, their own privacy policies and terms apply.

7. Data Sharing

The app does not sell your personal information. Data may be shared only with service providers or platforms necessary to operate core features, such as Apple for subscriptions and notification services, and backend infrastructure used to support account, alert, and wallet-tracking functionality.

8. Analytics and Tracking

Based on the current version of the app, Crypto Tracker does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking for advertising purposes.

9. Your Choices

You can manage your information and permissions by:

  • reviewing or changing notification permissions in iOS Settings,
  • choosing what wallet addresses, alerts, and tracked assets you save in the app,
  • signing out of your account, where supported, and
  • deleting the app if you want to remove locally stored app data from your device.

10. Children's Privacy

Crypto Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
