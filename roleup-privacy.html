Privacy Policy

App: RoleUp – Job Search
Effective date: June 2, 2026

RoleUp respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information the App Collects

RoleUp may collect or store the following information to provide its features:

  • Profile information you voluntarily enter, such as your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and LinkedIn URL, used to pre-fill job applications.
  • Resume (PDF), if you choose to upload one, stored locally on your device and used only for the Easy Apply feature.
  • Saved jobs, stored locally on your device.
  • Applied job IDs, stored locally on your device to track which roles you have applied to.
  • Search queries, sent to the RoleUp API to retrieve job listings. Queries are not linked to a personal account.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to operate and improve the app experience, including to:

  • search for and display real-time job listings from multiple sources,
  • pre-fill application forms when using Easy Apply,
  • save and display jobs you have bookmarked,
  • track roles you have applied to, and
  • remember your appearance preferences (light, dark, or system theme).

3. Data Storage

All profile data, saved jobs, applied job IDs, and resume files are stored locally on your device using iOS system storage. The app does not require you to create an account and does not upload your personal information to any server.

Search queries are transmitted to the RoleUp API solely to retrieve job results and are not stored against any personal identifier.

4. Third-Party Services

Job listings are sourced from third-party job boards and applicant tracking systems (such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Reed, and Adzuna). When you tap "Apply Now" and are redirected to a company's website or ATS, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Company logos may be fetched from Clearbit's Logo API for companies not in the app's built-in logo set. Clearbit may log these requests in accordance with their own privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing

RoleUp does not sell your personal information. The app does not share your name, email, phone number, LinkedIn URL, or resume with any third party, except as transmitted directly by you when completing a job application on an external website.

6. Your Choices

You can control your data at any time by:

  • editing or clearing your profile information within the app,
  • removing your uploaded resume within the app,
  • unsaving bookmarked jobs within the app, and
  • deleting the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

RoleUp is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
