Privacy Policy

App: SiteProof – Site Photo Documentation
Effective date: June 15, 2026

SiteProof respects your privacy. The app is designed to keep your site photos and project records on your own device and in your own private iCloud account. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information the App Handles

SiteProof handles the following information to provide its features:

  • Photos you capture in the app or import from your photo library, stored on your device.
  • Project details you enter, such as project name, client name, and site address.
  • Location data (GPS coordinates), with your permission, attached to photos so they can be stamped with where they were taken and matched to the nearest project. Location is only accessed while you are using the app.
  • Photo metadata such as capture date/time and device model, stamped on photos to support an accurate record.
  • Company / contractor details you optionally enter (name and contact) for inclusion on the PDF reports you generate.
  • Voice notes and captions you choose to add to photos.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to operate the app's features, including to:

  • capture, organize, and display your site photos by project,
  • stamp photos with their date, time, location, and address,
  • generate branded PDF reports that you choose to share, and
  • keep your data in sync across your own devices via iCloud.

3. Data Storage

Photos, projects, notes, tags, and your company details are stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud enabled for the app, this data is also synced to your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) under your Apple ID. The developer has no access to your private iCloud data. The app does not require you to create a separate account and does not upload your photos or project data to any developer-operated server.

4. Third-Party Services

SiteProof uses Apple system services only:

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit to sync your data within your own Apple account.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit) to convert GPS coordinates into a human-readable address for your photos. Coordinates are sent to Apple to perform this lookup, subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit to process optional Pro subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment details.

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

5. Data Sharing

SiteProof does not sell or share your personal information. PDF reports and exported files are shared only when you explicitly choose to share them using the iOS share sheet, to recipients you select.

6. Your Choices

You can control your data at any time by:

  • granting or denying camera and location access in iOS Settings,
  • editing or deleting projects, photos, and company details within the app,
  • using the in-app Storage tools to free up space or export and remove project data, and
  • deleting the app to remove locally stored data, and managing or deleting iCloud data from iCloud settings.

7. Children's Privacy

SiteProof is a tool for professionals and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
