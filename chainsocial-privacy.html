ChainSocial

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

ChainSocial is a wallet-first social network. Your public presence — posts, likes, follows, and reposts — is owned by you on the Lens Protocol and published on-chain. App utilities such as direct messages, bookmarks, lists, notifications, and settings are handled by ChainSocial to keep the experience fast. This policy explains what we process and why.

No passwords

ChainSocial does not use passwords. You sign in by connecting a wallet and signing a one-time challenge message. We never see or store your private keys or seed phrase. Wallet connection and signing are handled by your wallet provider (for example, Privy).

On-chain public content

Posts, replies, likes, follows, and reposts are recorded on the Lens Protocol and stored on public, decentralized infrastructure. This content is public by design and is generally permanent — it can be read by anyone and is not solely under ChainSocial's control. Your wallet address is your public identifier.

App-managed data

To provide centralized features, we store direct messages, bookmarks, lists, notification records, and display settings, associated with your wallet address. We process this data to operate those features and for safety and abuse prevention.

Media uploads

Images you upload are checked for type and safety, then stored and served through standard IPFS and media gateways (such as Pinata, ipfs.io, and arweave.net). Media attached to public posts is publicly accessible.

Safety and moderation

We scan content and media to enforce our rules and applicable law, and we keep records of reports and moderation actions. If you report a post, reply, message, or user, we store the report to review and act on it. See our Terms of Service for the rules that apply.

Third-party services

We rely on third parties to provide the service, including the Lens Protocol (on-chain social), Privy and WalletConnect (wallet authentication), IPFS/media gateways (media hosting), and our hosting and infrastructure providers. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand overall usage and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Regional availability

Some features may be limited or unavailable in certain regions to comply with local law. When that applies, the app will tell you.

Your choices and rights

You can request export or deletion of the app-managed data we hold about you (such as messages, bookmarks, lists, and settings) by contacting us. You manage blocked accounts in the app's settings.

Content published on-chain via Lens is controlled by you through the Lens Protocol and your wallet; because it lives on decentralized infrastructure, it may persist publicly even after you stop using ChainSocial.

Children

ChainSocial is not directed to children. You must meet the minimum age in our Terms of Service to use the app.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
